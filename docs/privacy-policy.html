Privacy Policy

Stashh · Last updated: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Stashh mobile application (“Stashh”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information. By using Stashh you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information — your email address and authentication credentials, used to create and secure your account.
  • Content you save — the links, videos, notes, titles, tags, transcripts and related metadata you choose to save to your library.
  • Usage data — basic technical information needed for the app to function (e.g. timestamps of saved items).

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, or advertising identifiers, and Stashh contains no third-party ads or ad tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and sync your saved library across your devices.
  • To analyze, summarize, transcribe and categorize the content you save, so it can be organized and searched.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the app.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver core features, content you save and related text may be processed by the following providers:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and storage (hosts your account and saved library).
  • OpenAI — generates titles, summaries, tags and semantic search data from the content you save. Submitted text is processed to produce these results.
  • Link metadata service — when you save a link, its public page data (title, thumbnail, captions) may be fetched to enrich your saved item.

These providers process data only to provide their function to Stashh and are bound by their own privacy and security terms. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in your account and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Access to your library is restricted to your authenticated account.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account exists. You may delete individual items in the app at any time. To delete your entire account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will process the request promptly.

6. Children’s Privacy

Stashh is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
